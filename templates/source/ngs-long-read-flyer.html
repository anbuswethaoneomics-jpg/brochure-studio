Long-read sequencing

Reads of thousands of bases resolve repeats, structural variants and full-length transcripts that short reads cannot. We run PacBio SMRT and Oxford Nanopore and switch between platforms as your research needs change.

PacBio SMRT sequencing

Single Molecule Real-Time sequencing watches fluorescently tagged nucleotides being added to individual DNA molecules in zero-mode waveguides. Platforms: PacBio RS II and Sequel.

Advantages
  • Longest average read lengths
  • Highest consensus accuracy
  • Uniform coverage
  • Simultaneous epigenetic characterization
  • Single-molecule resolution
Oxford Nanopore sequencing

DNA or RNA passes through nanopores and changes in ionic current identify each base, so the native strand is read directly without optics or amplification. Devices: Flongle, MinION, GridION, PromethION, SmidgION.

Advantages
  • Ultra-long reads of several hundred kb
  • Direct DNA and RNA sequencing
  • Real-time data streaming
  • Works with genomic DNA, cDNA and RNA
  • Epigenetic information from native strands
