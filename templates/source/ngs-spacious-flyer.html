Our services

Whole genome sequencing

De novo assembly and re-sequencing to find variation across the entire genome of microbes, humans, animals, plants and insects.

Whole exome sequencing

Focused sequencing of coding regions with SNV, InDel, CNV and trio analysis for clinical and disease research.

RNA and small RNA sequencing

Measure gene expression, discover novel transcripts and splice variants, and profile miRNA and single cells.

Epigenome sequencing

WGBS, MeDIP, ChIP-seq and ATAC-seq to study DNA methylation, binding sites and chromatin state.

Metagenome sequencing

Profile microbial communities from soil, host and environmental samples with 16S, 18S, ITS and shotgun methods.

Long-read sequencing

PacBio SMRT and Oxford Nanopore reads for genome finishing, structural variants and full-length transcripts.

Genotyping

SNP microarray, GBS and dd-RAD for GWAS, linkage maps, population studies and breeding programmes.

Bioinformatics analysis

Variant calling, annotation, assembly and custom pipelines run on your own dataset.
