LONG-READ SEQUENCING

Finish genomes. Resolve variants. Read what short reads miss.

Researchers still face challenges in finishing genomes, characterizing variants and recognizing the role of key biological markers. In many areas of research, accurate read lengths of 1,000 base pairs or longer in a single sequencing reaction are important. ONEOMICS long-read systems let you switch easily between platforms and applications as your research needs change.
