Methods

Four ways to map the epigenome

WGBS · RRBS · MeDIP

DNA methylation

Convert unmethylated cytosines with bisulfite, or pull down methylated DNA. Methylation is quantified across the target regions, such as CpG islands.

ChIP-seq

DNA binding sites

Immunoprecipitation with sequencing finds where proteins bind across the genome, with peaks annotated by position, read count, p-value and motif.

HISTONE CHIP-SEQ

Chromatin state

Antibodies against histone modifications reveal promoters, active and inactive enhancers and actively transcribed genes.

ATAC-seq

Open chromatin

Traces genome segments that are not densely packed, which are linked to active regions of gene expression or regulation.
