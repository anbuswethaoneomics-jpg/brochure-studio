Who we are

About ONEOMICS

ONEOMICS Private Limited is a genomics company delivering end-to-end solutions across sequencing services, sample collection and preservation products, nucleic-acid extraction and library-prep kits, and bioinformatics software.

From a single saliva sample to a full metagenome, our integrated portfolio helps researchers, clinicians and agri-scientists move from sample to insight with confidence.

Our Mission

To make high-quality genomic science accessible by providing reliable sequencing services, robust sample-stabilization products and dependable molecular tools.

Our Vision

To be a trusted partner in genomics, enabling discoveries that advance human health, agriculture and the environment.
