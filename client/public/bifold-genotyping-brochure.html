Technologies

Four ways to read genetic variation

01

Genotyping by sequencing (GBS)

Restriction enzymes and barcoded adapters shrink the genome to a representative fraction. High SNP coverage in gene-rich regions at low cost, with no reference sequence needed.

Low costNo fractionationLarge scale
02

dd-RAD sequencing

Type IIB enzymes cut fixed-size fragments, giving even sequencing depth and high reproducibility. Works with low DNA input and degraded DNA.

Flexible tag numberHigh marker density
03

SNP microarray

Custom, scalable Affymetrix and Illumina arrays with call rates above 99%. Measures SNPs and copy number variation at targeted or whole-genome scale.

High throughputClinical testingPharmacogenomics
04

Whole genome SNP genotyping

Microarray or high-throughput sequencing for genome-wide discovery and screening of SNP loci, supporting genome-wide association studies.

GWASGenome-wide discovery
